    How this reviewer is designed

    The layout follows the 8 principles of design. Balance: centered, symmetrical cards on a steady grid. Contrast: starlight yellow on night blue for what matters most. Emphasis: "Must-know" boxes and one bright primary button per screen. Movement: the Starry Night swirls and the numbered study path lead the eye forward. Pattern and Repetition: every topic, card and question uses the same parts in the same places. Rhythm: one spacing scale keeps a calm, even beat. Proportion and Hierarchy: size and weight show what to read first. Unity: one palette, one type pairing, one icon style.

    The study method combines active recall (flashcards, brain dumps, quick checks, recall mode), spaced repetition (cards return when you're about to forget them), and immediate feedback with explanations.
