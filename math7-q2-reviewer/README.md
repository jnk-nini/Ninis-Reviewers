# Mathematics 7 · Quarter 2 Reviewer

Covers the six lessons of the Q2 review sheet: roots of perfect squares and cubes, real numbers, converting units of measure, volume of a cylinder, volume of a pyramid, and sets.

Static site, no build step. Works offline through a service worker (`sw.js`) and can be installed to the home screen.

- `data_notes.js`: study notes, must-know lists, list-recall items, formula sheet, exam tips
- `data_bank.js`: question bank (multiple choice, true/false, identification) with clues and explanations; flashcards
- `gen.js`: generators for unlimited computation drills (each with a clue and a worked solution)
- `app.js`: the app (notes with Recall mode, expand all, listen mode / text-to-speech, quizzes, drills, flashcards, list recall, blurt test, mock exam, progress)
- `style.css`: styles (light and dark)
